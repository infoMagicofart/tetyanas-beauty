    Questions

    Good to know

    Something else? Message Tetyana on WhatsApp.

    Does waxing hurt?

    A little, mostly on the first visit. It gets easier with regular appointments as hair grows back finer.

    How long should my hair be?

    About 6 mm, the length of a grain of rice. Usually 2 to 3 weeks after shaving.

    How do I pay?

    Cash, debit or e-transfer after your appointment.

    Can I cancel or reschedule?

    Of course. Please let Tetyana know at least 24 hours before your visit.

    Is it OK during my period?

    Yes, though skin can be more sensitive. Tampons or a cup are fine for bikini services.
